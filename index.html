Privacy Policy

Stopwatch & Timer · Effective date: November 19, 2026

Stopwatch & Timer ("the app") is a simple stopwatch and countdown timer for Android, developed by Sa'Al. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no user accounts and does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

To keep working after you close it, the app saves your settings (sound, vibration, theme, keep-screen-on), your recent timer durations, and the current state of your stopwatch and timer, including laps. This data is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to show a "Timer finished" alert when a timer ends while the app is closed. You can decline it and the app still works.
  • Vibrate: used only to vibrate when a timer ends or when you tap buttons, and can be turned off in Settings.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or the internet.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The app contains no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
