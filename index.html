Privacy Policy for Digital Tasbeeh

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy applies to the Digital Tasbeeh Android app (package name com.digitaltasbeeh.counter), published by Sa'Al ("we", "us").

Summary

Digital Tasbeeh does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app works entirely offline.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or any other personal data. The app has no accounts or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves your counts, daily history, custom dhikr, targets and display settings in the app's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device. It is deleted when you use "Erase all data" in Settings or when you uninstall the app.

Internet access, permissions and third parties

The app does not request internet access or any other permission. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs, so no third party receives any data from the app.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a screen lock.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
